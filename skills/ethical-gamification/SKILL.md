# Ethical gamification and engagement

## When to use
Use for progression, achievements, XP, rewards and engagement systems.

## Workflow
- Define intrinsic learning or product goals before points and streaks.
- Prevent compulsive use, manipulative scarcity and punishment for missed days.
- Ensure fairness, privacy, anti-abuse checks and accessibility.
- Specify XP/level formulas, caps and recovery rules.
- Evaluate retention alongside wellbeing and learning outcomes; do not treat arbitrary thresholds as validated benchmarks.
