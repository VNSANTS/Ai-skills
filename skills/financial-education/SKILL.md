# Financial education and risk analysis

## When to use
Use for educational financial explanations, budgets, scenario analysis and risk framing.

## Workflow
- Separate verified facts, estimates, assumptions and opinions.
- Use current primary sources for rates, taxes, regulation and market data; cite dates.
- Show formulas and units, inflation, liquidity, fees and downside cases.
- Avoid guarantees, fabricated returns and personalized regulated advice.
- Explain uncertainty and educational purpose.
