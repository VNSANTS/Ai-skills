# Product discovery and delivery

## When to use
Use for product discovery, prioritization, roadmaps, pricing and delivery decisions.

## Workflow
- Define problem, target users and evidence.
- Distinguish assumptions from research findings.
- Prioritize by user value, effort, risk and cost.
- Define success and guardrail metrics before implementation.
- Keep roadmaps adaptable; evaluate pricing ethically and transparently.
- Prefer lean solutions and avoid unnecessary features.
- Track decisions, owners and validation status.
